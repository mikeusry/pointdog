## Why contractors are a perfect fit for this

Home service companies spend real money on Google, Local Services Ads, and Meta. Most of
their leads are phone calls. And the thing that matters (a booked job that turned into a
paid invoice) happens days later inside ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber, where the
ad platforms can't see it.

So Google optimizes for calls and form fills. Some are $12,000 generator installs. Some are
people asking if you sell light bulbs.

## What we build

**Revenue-backed conversions.** Booked, completed, and paid jobs sent to Google, Meta, and
Microsoft with invoice values, matched to the ad click or to hashed customer phone and email.
See [offline conversion tracking](/guides/offline-conversion-tracking).

**Audiences that update themselves.** Past customers excluded from new-customer ads. Unsold
estimates retargeted. Generator and HVAC owners (from installed equipment) reminded about
maintenance. Members excluded so you don't pay to reach people who already call you. See
[Customer Match and custom audiences](/guides/customer-match-custom-audiences).

**A website built from your jobs.** Service and town pages with real completed-job counts,
price ranges drawn from actual invoices, and reviews tied to specific work. Programmatic SEO
done right, which is what we did for Heiser Group across a dozen service cities.

**Call attribution that closes the loop.** Tracking numbers, booking widgets, and forms all
carry the ad source into the job record.

## Each platform is a little different

- **ServiceTitan** has a deep API and its own Marketing Pro suite that already covers Google
  and Meta for many contractors. Access for outside developers goes through certified apps or
  customer-built apps on higher tiers. Sharing API keys with a third party is prohibited.
- **Housecall Pro** offers API access on its higher plans.
- **Jobber** has a developer platform with a GraphQL API.

We scope which path fits your plan and your tools before promising anything.

## What we don't do

We don't replace your field software, and we won't try to talk you out of a tool that's
working. If your platform's built-in marketing features already send revenue to your ad
accounts, we'll tell you, and focus on the parts that are missing.

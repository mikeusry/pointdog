## The problem with renting your listings

Most brokerage websites show listings through an IDX widget. It's the same widget every
other broker in the market uses, it loads slowly, and the listings typically aren't real
pages on your domain, so they don't help you rank.

When you run Google Ads to those pages, the visitor waits for a frame to load, can't
deep-link a property, and bounces. You pay for the click either way.

## What we build

- **A direct MLS bridge.** Listings, photos, status changes, and open houses synced from your
  MLS's RESO Web API into your own database on a schedule (hourly for Hamilton Agency).
- **Real pages for every listing and neighborhood.** Indexed on your domain, fast, with
  proper structured data and share cards.
- **Search your visitors actually want.** Map search, neighborhood and school filters, saved
  searches, all on your site.
- **Listing data in your ads.** Current listings feeding landing pages and catalogs, so a
  campaign for a neighborhood lands on that neighborhood's live inventory.

## Housing ad rules, in plain English

Housing ads on Meta and Google come with special category rules that restrict audience
targeting by things like age, gender, and ZIP code. That makes the website and the listing
data more important, not less: when you can't narrow the audience much, the landing page has
to do the converting.

## Proof

Hamilton Agency, an Athens brokerage, runs on exactly this: a RESO-compliant listing database
with hourly sync and a photo CDN behind an Astro site. Every listing is a real page, and the
site ranks for neighborhood searches that iframe widgets can't touch.

## What Shopify's apps do well, and what they miss

Shopify's Google & YouTube app and its Meta app are worth installing. They send online
purchases from the browser and the server, and they sync your product catalog.

What they don't send:

- **Orders outside Shopify.** Phone orders, wholesale, trade accounts, anything keyed into
  another system.
- **Margin.** A $300 order at 15% margin and a $150 order at 60% margin look backwards to
  Google if you only send revenue.
- **Refunds and cancellations.** The platform keeps counting sales that came back.
- **Who's new and who's loyal.** Google and Meta should know when an order came from a
  first-time buyer versus your fifth-time customer. You probably want to pay more for the
  first one.
- **Lifetime value.** Which campaigns brought in customers who kept buying.

## We run this on our own brand first

Southland Organics, one of our brands, sells on Shopify and takes larger orders by phone in a
separate order system. When Google only saw Shopify, it optimized for small online orders.
Now phone orders and Shopify purchases feed Google as separate conversion actions from our
data warehouse, and customer segments sync to Google as Customer Match audiences.

## What we build

1. **Checkout capture.** Click IDs and UTMs saved to the order through Shopify's customer
   events, so every order carries its ad source.
2. **Order bridge.** A webhook or scheduled sync reads orders, refunds, and customer history
   and computes value (revenue or margin).
3. **Offline orders.** Orders from your other systems matched to the ad click or to hashed
   email and phone, uploaded daily.
4. **Platform sends.** [Google's Data Manager API](/guides/google-ads-data-manager-api),
   [Meta's Conversions API](/guides/meta-conversions-api), Microsoft offline conversions,
   and GA4.
5. **Audiences.** Customers, top spenders, lapsed buyers, and recent purchasers, synced
   daily. See [Customer Match and custom audiences](/guides/customer-match-custom-audiences).

## Beyond ads

The same order data can feed your email and SMS platform (segments by what people bought and
when) or another system entirely. For the CPRC ENOUGH! campaign, we pipe every Shopify order
into their donor CRM with influencer attribution in real time.

## The Salesforce attribution gap

The click happens on a lead. The revenue happens on an opportunity weeks later, often after
the lead has been converted, merged, or reassigned. Somewhere in that journey the original
ad click gets lost, and the ad platforms never learn which campaigns produced revenue.

## What we build

1. **Capture.** Click IDs and hashed contact info stored on the Salesforce lead from every
   web form and booking tool. See [click IDs explained](/guides/click-ids-gclid-fbclid-msclkid).
2. **Follow the lead.** Converted leads traced to their contact and opportunity, so the click
   ID follows the money.
3. **Stage conversions.** Opportunity stage changes (qualified, proposal, closed-won) sent as
   conversions with the opportunity amount or a predicted value for early stages.
4. **Every platform.** Google through the
   [Data Manager API](/guides/google-ads-data-manager-api), Meta through the
   [Conversions API](/guides/meta-conversions-api), and Microsoft offline conversions, from
   one pipeline.
5. **Audiences.** Customers excluded from prospecting campaigns, closed-won accounts as
   lookalike seeds.

Plenty of 10- and 20-person companies run Salesforce without a Salesforce admin, let alone a

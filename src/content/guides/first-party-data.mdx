## What it is

**First-party data** is what your business knows about its own customers because they told
you or bought from you: names, emails, phone numbers, orders, jobs, invoices, bookings,
memberships, and what they did on your site with consent.

It's different from third-party data (bought from brokers or inferred by platforms), and
it's the most valuable data you'll ever have for marketing.

## Why it decides ad performance now

Ad platforms used to watch people across the internet and figure out who your customers were.
They see much less now:

- Safari and Firefox block third-party cookies.
- iOS asks permission before apps can track, and most people say no.
- Ad blockers and privacy tools are everywhere.

So Google and Meta lean on what advertisers send them directly. The business that sends
*"this click became a $3,800 customer"* beats the one that sends *"someone filled out a
form"* every time.

## What you already have

Most small businesses are sitting on excellent first-party data. It's just in software the
marketing never touches:

| Where it lives | What it knows |
|---|---|
| Store (Shopify, WooCommerce, Square) | Orders, margins, repeat purchases, refunds |
| CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive) | Leads, deal stages, closed revenue |
| Field service (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber) | Booked jobs, invoices, equipment, memberships |
| Scheduling and practice software | Appointments, show rates, repeat visits |
| Accounting (QuickBooks, Xero) | Who actually paid |

## Putting it to work

**1. Capture the link to the ad.** Store the click ID and contact info with every lead. See
[click IDs explained](/guides/click-ids-gclid-fbclid-msclkid).

**2. Keep one clean record per customer.** Standardized emails and phones, merged
duplicates, lifetime value, last purchase. This is what people mean by a CDP.

**3. Send outcomes to the ad platforms.** Revenue-backed conversions to Google, Meta, and
Microsoft. See [offline conversion tracking](/guides/offline-conversion-tracking).

**4. Build audiences that update themselves.** Exclusions, lookalikes, win-backs. See
[Customer Match and custom audiences](/guides/customer-match-custom-audiences).

**5. Use it on your own site and email.** Pages built from real records, emails segmented by
what people actually bought.

## How we do it for our own companies

Southland Organics, one of our brands, pulls Shopify, GA4, email, call tracking, and CRM data
into one BigQuery warehouse. From there, automated syncs push lifetime value and customer
segments back into HubSpot for the sales team and send conversions to the ad platforms. Same
pattern we build for clients, sized to the business.

## You don't need an enterprise CDP

The enterprise customer data platforms are built for companies with data teams. A small
business needs the same result (one clean customer record, feeding ads and email) with a
fraction of the machinery: a database, a few automated syncs, and someone who understands
both the APIs and the marketing.

## Why your customer list is your best targeting

Every ad platform is trying to guess who your customers are. You already know. Uploading that
list, hashed and consented, lets you:

- **Exclude current customers** from new-customer campaigns, so you stop paying to reach
  people who would have called you anyway.
- **Seed lookalikes** from your best customers, not all customers.
- **Win back** people who haven't bought in a year.
- **Follow up** on unsold estimates, abandoned quotes, and expired memberships.

## The groups worth building

| Group | Use it for |
|---|---|
| All customers | Exclude from acquisition campaigns |
| Top 20% by lifetime value | Lookalike and similar-audience seeds |
| Bought once, not in 12 months | Win-back offers |
| Open estimates or quotes | Retargeting with follow-up or financing offers |
| Active members or subscribers | Exclude, or cross-sell only |
| Customers in the last 30 days | Review and referral asks |

These should come straight from your system of record: store, CRM, or field-service
software, not a spreadsheet someone exports twice a year.

## Google Customer Match: rules to know

- **Eligibility:** good policy compliance and payment history. Some features need 90+ days of
  account history and more than $50,000 lifetime spend.
- **Size:** at least 100 active members. Memberships older than 540 days stop counting unless
  they're refreshed.
- **Format:** emails, phones, names, and addresses, normalized and hashed with SHA-256.
- **Where uploads go:** audience uploads are available through Google's Data Manager API,
  the same place offline conversions now go. See
  [Google's Data Manager API](/guides/google-ads-data-manager-api).

## Meta custom audiences: rules to know

- **Customer list audiences** from hashed emails and phones, plus extra identifiers for
  better matching.
- **Lookalikes** need a source of at least 100 people from one country. A smaller list of
  high-value customers usually beats a big list of everyone.
- **Special ad categories** (housing, employment, credit, social issues) limit targeting
  and lookalikes.

## Why daily sync beats CSV uploads

A list uploaded in March is wrong by May. New customers are still in your acquisition
campaigns, and last month's buyers are getting win-back ads. An automated daily sync keeps
every group current with no one remembering to do it.

## Consent and privacy

Only upload people who gave you their information directly and were told how it's used. Say
so in your privacy policy. Both platforms require it, and it's the right way to treat
customers.
